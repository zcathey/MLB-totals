Picks are logged automatically on the morning of each game from live lines, skipping flagged games. CLV = closing line value in points: positive means the pick beat the last line seen before kickoff. Over a small sample it's a better test of the model than wins and losses.

BY WEEK

EVERY GAME

Each week is graded with ratings built only from the games before it, against nflverse's closing spread and total. That makes it a fair out-of-sample test, but closing lines are the hardest number to beat, so expect results near 50%.
Ratings are yards per play above or below average, adjusted for every opponent's strength (offense and defense solved together). OVR = OFF + DEF. Points are projected per game against an average opponent: 23.0 + 6.01 × rating. MARGIN and TO are actual points and turnover margin per game, shown for context; the model doesn't use them. Click a column to sort.
Strength of schedule = average adjusted rating of each team's opponents (higher = tougher). DEF FACED = quality of the defenses its offense has played; OFF FACED = quality of the offenses its defense has played.
