## How to read this page

- The model only knows season-long yards per play, adjusted for opponents. It can't see injuries, weather, quarterback changes or why a team's points don't match its yards.
- The **Backtest** tab grades every week since Week 4 using only the games before it, against closing lines. That's the honest test of whether the edges mean anything.
- **CLV** on the Picks tab shows whether the market moved toward the model after a pick was logged. Over a few weeks it's a better signal than wins and losses.
