**Play: Lions +1.5.** I'd still bet it down to pick'em.

The line moved from Bears −2.5 on Monday to −1.5 on DraftKings by Wednesday ([SI, Nov 25](https://www.si.com/betting/bears-vs-lions-odds-move-toward-detroit-ahead-of-thanksgiving-day-matchup-01m8a9ezhr3c)) and is −1 at Pinnacle this morning. SI's writer thinks sharp money on Detroit is more likely than hidden injury news. That's his read, but I couldn't find any news to explain the move either. It's under 2 points, and both QBs are off the injury report.

Why I'm still careful: a 10-point edge is huge, and it usually means the model is missing something. The market's case for Chicago is its record, Detroit's injuries (DE Aidan Hutchinson on IR, C Cade Mays to IR on Nov 21), and the short week ([Detroit Sports Nation via Yahoo, Nov 22](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/detroit-lions-vs-chicago-bears-014533398.html)). But both teams played Sunday, and Hutchinson was already out last week. The model rates Detroit 5th and Chicago 20th on opponent-adjusted yards per play. The market has been moving toward the model all week, which is the condition for a play. One "agrees" pick so far (a total, 1-0) is no evidence the rule works. It's the rule, not a track record.

Injuries ([Roundtable via Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/articles/bears-vs-lions-latest-injury-001516019.html), [Detroit Sports Nation, Nov 26](https://detroitsportsnation.com/chicago-bears-rule-out-three-players-detroit-lions-thanksgiving/richardknight/detroit-lions/11/26/2026/510805/)):
